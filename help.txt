bg — Bro Gang Package Manager

Usage:
  bg install <pkg>[@version]
  bg uninstall <pkg>
  bg list
  bg upgrade [pkg]
  bg search <query>
  bg info <pkg>
  bg repo add <name> <url>
  bg repo update
  bg doctor
  bgcreate                    Interactive manifest generator

Aliases: bg i / bg rm / bg ls / bg up
Home:    %USERPROFILE%\.broGang  (override: BROGANG_HOME)
